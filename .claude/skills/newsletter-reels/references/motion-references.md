# Motion references

The motion-design reels the owner pointed at on 2026-10-25 (two from @motionlovee on TikTok, with captions claiming a model made them). They are the bar for the Motion-max arm. Shot lists are inferred from frames captured 0.5 s apart, so exact easing and beat sync are not known.

## Reference 1: AI-doom lyric video (38 s)
The film plays on a dark laptop on a glossy black table, lit only by its screen, with a mirror reflection. Near-black slate ground, one hot orange accent with bloom, white heavy grotesk words, small mono labels, grey hairline linework. HUD furniture in every shot: corner crop marks, a hairline grid, a code or coordinates column, tiny readouts. A new line every 1-2 s, cut on the beat.

1. 0-1 s: a hand taps the laptop and the screen wakes (the hook).
2. 1-5 s: a construction grid with a circle and radial guides; an orange glowing rectangle is measured with dimension arrows; line art builds stroke by stroke into a figure while matching code types in on the left.
3. 5-10 s: one giant word stamped full width; small words type in one by one beside it; an orange comet head traces each stroke as it draws.
4. 10-14 s: an orange line chart draws on, then the camera flies through it.
5. 14-22 s: a topographic field of wobbly concentric rings with a glowing orange path through it; a word resolves letter by letter; words rotate 90 degrees and wrap around the ring centre.
6. 22-26 s: a terminal prompt types a full sentence with an orange block cursor over the pulsing rings; some words flip to inverted orange; then hard cuts to full-screen stamps, one a number in glowing mono whose last digit is caught mid-roll like an odometer.
7. 25-29 s: a phrase beside a branching orange lightning network; a word whose O's are drawn as concentric rings.
8. 29-31 s: a wireframe 3D corridor fly-through with words set into its walls.
9. 31-38 s: a glitched mask on a datamoshed ground, then a hatched 3D creature with orange eyes and HUD callout boxes on leader lines pointing at kinetic words, with mono timecodes. It holds on that composition.

Count: about 15 distinct states in 38 s, never fewer than 3 moving layers, transitions mixing hard cuts, draw-on reveals and camera fly-throughs.

## Reference 2: editor fly-through (18 s)
The same laptop framing. On screen a virtual camera flies through a 3D-tilted motion-design editor: dark UI, a white artboard, one orange accent, a strip of coloured layer bars, heavy motion blur and shallow depth of field.

1. 0-2 s: a small voxel mascot drops onto the tilted artboard.
2. 2-4 s: it scribbles a mark that resolves into the brand logo.
3. 4-8 s: the camera pulls back and banks across the editor panels; a "made with" frame appears with fat 3D ribbon swooshes.
4. 8-14 s: the mascot runs along the timeline while the coloured layer bars extrude into 3D slabs it runs on, with whip pans and blur.
5. 14-18 s: the logo blooms over a starfield and settles on an end card.

What carries over: UI treated as a 3D set, a character or object leading the eye through it, a logo resolved out of a scribble, whip pans carrying blur, flat UI extruded into slabs.
