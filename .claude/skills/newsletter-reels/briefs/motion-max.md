
## Motion-max (experiment arm: this reel goes further than Motion-first)
This reel is in the experiment arm that tests whether even more motion wins. Read `{{SKILL}}/references/motion-references.md` before you write the beat map: those shot lists are the bar, so your film should feel like them, not like a faster explainer.
- **At least 12 distinct visual states in 25-30 s.** A state is a new composition, not the same layout with new text. List them with timestamps in `beatmap.md`.
- **At least 3 moving layers on every frame:** a foreground subject, a living mid layer (field, grid, particles, readouts) and a background or camera move. Nothing on screen is ever fully still, the end card included (a slow drift or a breathing light is enough there).
- **A camera move in every scene:** dolly, orbit, push-through, rack focus or a parallax pan.
- **Use at least 3 kinds of transition:** a hard cut on the beat, a match cut (a shape in one shot becomes a shape in the next), a masked wipe, a whip pan with motion blur, or a scale-through (the camera flies into an object to reach the next scene).
- **craft.md's "nothing cuts" rule does not apply to you.** Hard cuts on the beat are part of the vocabulary. Keep its carries for the moments where a match cut is the point.
- In REPORT.md, give your counts of states, layers and transition kinds, so the owner's pick can be compared against them.
