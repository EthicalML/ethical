AI should extend what people can understand and accomplish without quietly transferring responsibility to a system. Human oversight must be informed, timely, and capable of changing an outcome.

## Where it fails

Human augmentation fails when a tool narrows the choices people can see, obscures uncertainty, or makes an automated recommendation feel final.

- Automation displaces meaningful human judgement.
- Interfaces conceal uncertainty or system limitations.
- Efficiency targets override human wellbeing.

## Practical controls

Controls should keep accountable people close to consequential decisions and make it easy to understand why a recommendation was made.

- Keep accountable people in consequential decisions.
- Show confidence, limits, and routes to challenge.
- Measure outcomes for the people affected.
