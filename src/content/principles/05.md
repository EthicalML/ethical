Automating medium- or large-scale processes can affect many people across an organisation or industry. Technologists should look beyond the technology and help relevant stakeholders understand and document those effects.

Even when technologists do not lead an operational transformation, they should help ensure that appropriate change-management processes are in place. Those processes should mitigate impacts on workers regardless of the type of work being automated.
