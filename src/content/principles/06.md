Systems that learn from data need measures of accuracy that reflect the application in which they operate. Plain accuracy or default cost metrics may not capture what is correct or harmful from a human and domain-specific perspective.

Teams should examine the consequences of different errors and select practical measures such as precision, recall, F1 scores, or domain-specific cost functions. Domain knowledge should shape both evaluation and the relative cost assigned to each kind of error.
