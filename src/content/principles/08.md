---
number: "08"
title: Security and data risk
commitment: We commit to develop and improve reasonable processes and infrastructure to ensure data and model security are taken into consideration during the development of machine learning systems.
failure_modes:
  - Unrestricted model endpoints
  - Artifact exploit injection
  - Supply-chain vulnerabilities
controls:
  - MLSecOps controls
  - IAM and RBAC for ML services
  - Observability and lineage
---

Security is a lifecycle responsibility spanning data collection, model development, deployment, and retirement. Controls should match the system's exposure and the harm a compromise could cause.

Teams should assume that models and their surrounding tools will be probed by capable adversaries, then validate layered safeguards through repeatable testing and monitoring.
