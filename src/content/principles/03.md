Complex machine learning pipelines can produce results without giving technologists a clear understanding of how those results were reached. We should invest reasonable effort in tools and processes that explain results through the features and models chosen.

Domain knowledge can make even complex systems more explainable, including by encoding relevant knowledge in features rather than leaving models to infer it alone. Where this reduces accuracy, the trade-off should be weighed against the gains in transparency and explainability.
