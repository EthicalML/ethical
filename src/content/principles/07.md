Large-scale systems that learn from data can affect many stakeholders directly and indirectly. Building trust requires communicating what data is held, how it is handled, and why protecting it matters.

Privacy should be designed into both the system and its operating processes. Those protections should account for direct users, operational users, procurement stakeholders and others affected by the system.
