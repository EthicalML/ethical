An aligned system should behave consistently with the purpose, constraints and values established by accountable people. A system can meet a narrow metric while producing a harmful wider outcome.

Teams should make intent testable, examine how systems behave under pressure, and retain the ability to interrupt or revise deployments as real-world evidence changes.
