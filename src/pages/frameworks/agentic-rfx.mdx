## What the extension will address

The extension will request evidence for agent mandates and oversight under [P01 Human Augmentation by Oversight](/principles/01/), alignment with intent under [P09 Human Alignment by Intent](/principles/09/), and agentic security aligned to the ASI taxonomy under [P08 Safety by Security](/principles/08/). This closes the classic framework's known ninth-principle gap without changing its established criteria.

The criteria are being drafted against the nine principles. They are not yet ratified.
