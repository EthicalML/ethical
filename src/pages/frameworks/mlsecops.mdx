MLSecOps connects common machine-learning security failures to controls that teams can apply across data, model, deployment, and monitoring workflows.

## The Top 10

The framework gives security and ML teams a shared vocabulary for prioritising risks. Each category links an OWASP-style failure mode to concrete practices applied throughout the lifecycle.

## From taxonomy to practice

Teams can use the mappings during threat modelling, supplier review, release approval and post-deployment monitoring. They can then record evidence against the controls they adopt.
