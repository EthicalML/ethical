The AI-RFX Procurement Framework is an open-source set of RFP and RFI templates that converts The 9 Responsible AI Principles into requirements, evaluation criteria and supplier evidence for AI procurement.

## Beyond the model itself

AI-RFX evaluates the maturity of the technical infrastructure and processes around a system, not just its algorithms. Underneath it sits the [Machine Learning Maturity Model](/frameworks/maturity-model/), which turns each criterion into levels of organisational capability.

## The ninth principle

The framework predates the ninth principle, [Human Alignment by Intent](/principles/09/), and its eight criteria do not yet cover it. Alignment criteria are agentic by nature: they concern what a system is pursuing, not just how it is built. These criteria are being drafted as part of the [Agentic RFX Framework](/frameworks/agentic-rfx/).

## RFP and RFI templates

AI-RFX supports two stages of procurement. A Request for Information surveys the supplier landscape and tests what the market can provide. A Request for Proposal runs the procurement against defined requirements, evaluation criteria and supplier evidence. The templates are guides rather than one-size-fits-all documents, so teams can adapt their level of detail to the system and procurement.

The framework and templates are published under the MIT licence, allowing organisations and suppliers to reuse, modify and contribute to them.
