  checklist:
    - criterion: Practical benchmarks
      evidence: Use-case measures beyond accuracy, with processing time, training resources and operating requirements
    - criterion: Explainability by justification
      evidence: A proportionate account of system outputs, supported by audit trails and domain expertise
    - criterion: Data and model assessment
      evidence: Processes for identifying, documenting and mitigating unwanted bias under production conditions
    - criterion: Reproducible operations
      evidence: Model versioning, rollback, diagnosis, redeployment and separated development and serving workflows
    - criterion: Privacy-enforcing infrastructure
      evidence: Personal-data controls, consent handling, privacy documentation and re-identification safeguards
    - criterion: Operational process design
      evidence: Fail-safe steps, human review and escalation based on the impact of incorrect outputs
    - criterion: Change-management capabilities
      evidence: Rollout, workforce impact, training and handover plans for affected stakeholders
    - criterion: Security risk processes
      evidence: Access control, data protection, secured infrastructure and operator education
  capabilities:
    - capability: Benchmarking
      detail: Measures reflect the use case, error costs, time and resource requirements.
    - capability: Justification
      detail: Operators can inspect an appropriate account of how outputs were produced.
    - capability: Assessment
      detail: Data and model behaviour are evaluated for unwanted bias and production change.
    - capability: Reproducibility
      detail: Models and operations can be versioned, repeated, diagnosed and rolled back.
    - capability: Privacy
      detail: Personal data is separated, protected and governed according to consent.
    - capability: Operations
      detail: Human review and fail-safe steps are designed around the impact of errors.
    - capability: Change management
      detail: Rollout, training and handover account for the people affected by automation.
    - capability: Security
      detail: Models, data, infrastructure and operating processes are protected together.

## How the frameworks connect

The maturity model is what [AI-RFX](/frameworks/ai-rfx/) scores against: each procurement criterion asks where the organisation sits on the model's capability levels. Both inherit their targets from the [nine Responsible AI Principles](/principles/).

Agent identity, mandate boundaries and action observability are being drafted as part of the [Agentic Maturity Model](/frameworks/agentic-maturity-model/).
