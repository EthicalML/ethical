## ACM

**Role:** GOVERNING BOARD

ACM's Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct and its technology-policy work set expectations for computing professionals broadly. The nine Responsible AI Principles are a narrower, ML-specific complement — commitments a practitioner or organisation can act on day to day, alongside the professional-conduct baseline ACM sets.

## ISO / IEC

**Role:** AI STANDARDS GROUP

ISO/IEC's AI standards work sets the formal, cross-industry baseline for how AI systems are assessed and governed. The Institute's practical frameworks — AI-RFX and the ML Maturity Model — are built to be compatible with that baseline rather than to replace it.
