## ACM

**Role:** GOVERNING BOARD

ACM's Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct and its technology-policy work set expectations for computing professionals broadly. The nine Responsible AI Principles are a narrower, ML-specific complement — commitments a practitioner or organisation can act on day to day, alongside the professional-conduct baseline ACM sets.
{/* <!-- PROPOSAL: pending owner copy review --> */}

## Linux Foundation

**Role:** ASSOCIATE MEMBER

The Institute's collaboration with Linux Foundation AI & Data runs through two open-source projects: Kompute, the cross-vendor GPU acceleration work maintained alongside LF AI & Data and NumFOCUS, and the MLSecOps Top 10, which was connected to LF's Trusted AI work and an ML-security committee.

## ISO / IEC

**Role:** AI STANDARDS GROUP

The Institute participates in ISO/IEC AI standards work, which sets the formal, cross-industry baseline for how AI systems are assessed and governed. Its practical frameworks — AI-RFX and the ML Maturity Model — are built to be compatible with that baseline rather than to replace it.
