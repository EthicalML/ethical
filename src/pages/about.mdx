The Institute for Ethical AI Alignment & Safety is an independent research centre working on practical ways to keep advanced AI systems safe, accountable, and aligned with legitimate human intent.

## Our mission

We turn broad commitments about responsible AI into methods that engineers, organisations, standards bodies, and public institutions can use. Our aim is to make alignment and safety concrete enough to test, govern, and improve.

## What we do

We develop open frameworks, technical research, evaluation practices, and shared resources across The 9 Responsible AI Principles. The work spans agent oversight, model security, explainability, procurement, production monitoring, and institutional assurance.

## How we work

Our projects are built with practitioners and reviewed across technical, legal, policy, and social perspectives. We publish reusable artefacts, seek evidence from real deployments, and revise our guidance when assumptions no longer hold.

## Standards & affiliations

The Institute contributes to international discussions through work with the United Nations, UN CSTD, European Commission, ACM, Linux Foundation, OWASP, IEEE, and ISO/IEC communities. Participation informs our research without replacing independent judgement.
