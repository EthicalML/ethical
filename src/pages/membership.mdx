## Who it's for

- Founders and teams building AI products in startups and scale-ups.
- Professionals procuring, developing or operating AI systems.
- Professors and academics working on AI, data, privacy or machine learning.
- Engineers and data scientists designing, building or maintaining systems.
- Product, project and delivery managers responsible for stages of the AI system lifecycle.
- Leaders working across AI, alignment and data.

## How it works

Apply through the shared contact form with the network interest selected. The Institute reviews applications and follows up using the details provided.
